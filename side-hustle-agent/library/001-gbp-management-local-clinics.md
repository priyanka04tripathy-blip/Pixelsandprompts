## The offer
Done-for-you profile setup and monthly management for a local business with one location. It covers category and services cleanup, 8 to 12 photos, 2 posts a week, review replies, a review-ask routine, and a one-page monthly report.
Buyers: dental and skin clinics, gyms, salons, coaching centres and restaurants in tier-2 cities, especially ones with weak profiles.

## Why it could fail
1. Commodity pricing. IndiaMART sellers quote ₹1,000 to ₹5,000, so owners compare you with the cheapest quote. You must sell outcomes (calls and direction requests) and not "posting".
2. No visible results for 30 to 60 days. Local ranking moves slowly, so churn is likely if you promise rankings.
3. Policy risk. Keyword-stuffed names, fake reviews and bought reviews can get a profile suspended. Never do these.
