# SAMPLE deliverable: Google Business Profile audit + 30-day plan
Client: "Sample Dental Care, Rourkela" (a made-up clinic, used to test the workflow. Not a real business, and nothing here is real performance data.)

This is what Priyanka would hand a real client after a free audit call. It was produced by the agent as a dry-run of the workflow.

## 1. 15-point audit (fill in live from the client's actual profile)
| # | Check | How to check (2 min) | Typical fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Primary category is the most specific one ("Dental clinic", not "Doctor") | Profile > Edit profile > Business category | Change the primary, add up to 3 to 5 secondary categories |
| 2 | Business name matches the signboard exactly | Compare with the photo of the signboard | Remove keyword stuffing (it risks suspension) |
| 3 | Address pin is on the building entrance | Zoom in on the map | Drag the pin, verify |
| 4 | Phone number is answered during listed hours | Call it once | Add a WhatsApp number too |
| 5 | Hours are correct, including festival days | Read the hours section | Add special hours before Diwali, Holi and Chhath |
| 6 | Services list is filled, with descriptions | Edit profile > Services | Add every treatment, one line each |
| 7 | Description uses the full 750 characters naturally | Read it | Rewrite: who, what, where, how to book |
| 8 | At least 10 recent photos (exterior, interior, team, work) | Photos tab | Shoot 15 photos on a phone, upload |
| 9 | Review count and rating versus the 3 nearest competitors | Search "dentist near me" in the city | Build a review-ask routine |
| 10 | Owner replies to the last 10 reviews | Reviews tab | Reply to all, in 24 hours going forward |
| 11 | Posts in the last 30 days | Updates tab | Run the 30-day calendar below |
| 12 | Booking or appointment link present | Edit profile > Booking | Add a WhatsApp or form link |
| 13 | Q&A seeded with the 5 most common questions | Profile Q&A | Self-answer FAQs |
| 14 | Website link carries UTM tags | Click the link, check the URL | Add `?utm_source=gbp` |
| 15 | No duplicate or old listings | Search the phone number | Request removal or merge |

## 2. Review-ask message (WhatsApp, to send after each visit)
> Hi [Name], thank you for visiting [Clinic] today. If your experience was good, a short Google review helps other patients in Rourkela find us. Here is the one-tap link: [review link]. Thank you!

## 3. Review reply templates
- 5-star: "Thank you, [Name]! Glad your [treatment] went well. See you at your next check-up."
- 3-star or below: "Thank you for telling us, [Name]. We are sorry the visit fell short on [issue]. Please call [number] so we can make it right." Never argue in public, and never mention clinical details.
- Medical-privacy rule: never confirm that the reviewer was a patient or name the treatment in a reply unless the reviewer already stated it.

## 4. 30-day post calendar (2 posts per week plus 1 offer, as a starting plan)
| Week | Post | Format |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Meet the doctor and her qualifications | Photo + 3 lines |
| 1 | "3 signs you need a root canal" | Text + CTA to call |
| 1 | Offer: free check-up week | Offer post |
| 2 | Before/after (with written patient consent only) | Photo |
| 2 | "How often to replace a toothbrush" | Tip |
| 2 | Clinic tour | 15-sec video |
| 3 | Myth vs fact: whitening | Tip |
| 3 | Patient FAQ: braces cost range | Text |
| 3 | Offer: school-kids check-up | Offer post |
| 4 | Festival hours notice | Update |
| 4 | "What to do in a dental emergency" | Tip |
| 4 | Month recap + review thank-you | Update |

## 5. Monthly report skeleton (what the client sees)
Profile views | Searches | Calls | Direction requests | Website clicks | New reviews | Average rating | Posts published | Next month's focus.
Source: GBP Performance tab, screenshot attached. All numbers come from the client's own account.

## What this dry-run proves and does not prove
Proves: the deliverable can be produced in about 90 minutes using Claude plus the client's own photos, and it is specific enough to be usable.
Does NOT prove: that a clinic will pay for it, what the conversion rate is, or that rankings improve. Those need real pitches.
